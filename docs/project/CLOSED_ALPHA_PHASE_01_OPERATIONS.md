# Skadyn Idle — Closed Alpha Phase 01 Operations

## Objective

Operate the first Closed Alpha phase with a controlled group of 5 to 10 invited testers.

This document defines operational preparation only. It does not enable deferred gameplay capabilities, deployment or public access.

## Tester onboarding

- Invite only approved Alpha Testers.
- Grant Alpha Tester access manually.
- Confirm tester understands the phase objective: gameplay validation and feedback collection.
- Provide known limitations before access.
- Keep staff channels and internal tools restricted.

## Tester feedback flow

Primary channels:

- `#general` for discussion and casual feedback.
- `#help` for questions requiring follow-up.
- `#feedback` for structured suggestions.
- `#bug-reports` for reproducible issues.
- `#support` for private account or access problems.

These are **planned channel names/routing**, not a claim that Discord channels,
bot/ticket integrations, permissions or support staffing are currently
provisioned. Before tester invitations, complete and verify the separate
`DISCORD_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST_V1.md` and `DISCORD_COMMUNITY_OPERATIONS_V1.md`
operator gates as applicable; TASK-136 E0–E4 and Human live review remain mandatory.

Feedback classification:

- Gameplay blocker.
- Progression/balance issue.
- UX issue.
- Bug reproduction.
- Suggestion for future scope.

## Known limitations Phase 01

Deferred capabilities:

- Public CombatPresentation feed.
- Durable terminal/offline summary.
- Historical manual-capture compatibility changes.
- Move editor / eligible Moves authority.
- Pixi combat presentation.

These limitations are intentional scope boundaries.

## QA evidence collection

During Phase 01 collect:

- reproduction steps;
- affected account/tester context;
- version/build identifier;
- screenshots or logs without sensitive data;
- severity classification.

## Exit review

Before expanding scope, review:

- unresolved critical issues;
- player progression behavior;
- data consistency;
- tester feedback themes;
- next capability priorities.
