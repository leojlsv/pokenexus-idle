## 1. Boundary and approval

The Human Owner accepted the detailed Class-A public Combat-presentation contract on 2026-09-29, following independent QA and security/persistence contract reviews. This acceptance authorizes scoping a separate READY backend implementation task. It does **not** prove that the existing retained runtime already publishes a complete historical transcript, approve an unreviewed migration implementation, or authorize endpoint enablement, production rollout or Git-history operations.

**Enablement is a separate hard gate.** No production HTTP route, advertised capability, real Hunt event Card, or event-feed consumer may be activated until the immutable-origin evidence, pinned owned-Shiny identity, complete deterministic transcript equivalence, bounded early/late page work, and cross-restart/cursor/security tests in sections 6–10 are demonstrated. Unsupported or unprovable Hunt histories fail closed. An isolated TASK-028 fixture is a renderer test, never evidence that a real Hunt has a public transcript.

This spec adds a read-only, presentation-only transport to SPEC-015. It does not alter combat, capture, encounter rewards, elapsed-time advancement, healing, RNG, policy, replay ordering or any existing Hunt command response. The server is the exclusive authority for source events and identities.

## 2. Canonical representation

Every published combat envelope uses **the existing** `@pokenexus/game-protocol` TASK-028 `pokenexus.combat-presentation.v1` schema and its current bootstrap/continuation projectors. No Hunt-specific CombatEvent union, newly synthesized BattleStarted, wildcard HP display, or renderer-specific interpretation is allowed.

- A distinct Battle is identified by its stable `(huntId, encounterOrdinal, encounterId, battleId)`. Its first published envelope is exactly one `bootstrap` beginning with the authoritative `BattleStarted` event at `sequence=1`, `combatTimeMs=0`; initial sides contain the original ordered roster and original active memberships. All participants have their original Battle identities. Owned Pokémon include the immutable at-Hunt-start Shiny fact and exact **battle-origin** owned `currentHp`/`maxHp`. Wild Pokémon expose original visible species/form, level, Shiny and `conscious|ko` only.
- Subsequent slices of that Battle use only TASK-028 `continuation` envelopes and their HP-free producer context. Within a Battle, sequences are contiguous and time never decreases. A replacement may activate a pinned reserve in the same Battle; it does not create a new bootstrap.
- The next Battle begins a new bootstrap and resets its own CombatEvent sequence. The sole public cross-Battle ordering key is `(encounterOrdinal, battleId, event.sequence)`, with ordinal ordering authoritative; event time alone is not a unique key. Zero-gap Battles and events with equal logical times preserve this ordering.
- Source events retain TASK-028's privacy projection. The public protocol never directly includes exact wild HP, maximum HP, ratios/buckets, wild damage/heal amounts, hidden IV/Genetics/Profiles, seeds, RNG, individualization keys, source checkpoint bytes, or producer continuation context. A cross-side or ambiguous HP consequence remains hidden in TASK-028's **individual event field** projection. **The projection is not a guarantee of non-inference from multiple public snapshots.** In particular, TASK-028 demands a fresh exact owned-HP bootstrap for every Battle. Under a zero-gap transition, bootstrap for Battle N may reveal owned `currentHp=100`, and bootstrap for N+1 `currentHp=87`; if N had one identifiable hidden wild-source hit and no other owned-HP consequence, the exact 13 HP loss can be inferred even without a separate state GET. The accepted SPEC-015 state read also discloses committed exact owned HP and can corroborate this inference. Thus a feed containing all successive Battle bootstraps **cannot** assert that it keeps each individual wild-source damage amount secret. Its guarantee is narrower: protected wild **vitality/Genetics/provenance fields are never emitted numerically** and individual sensitive event fields remain masked.
- **MVP product decision — accepted:** the Human Owner accepts that comparing exact **owned** HP across Battle bootstraps and committed state GETs may reveal aggregate or even individual wild-to-owned damage. This inference does **not** authorize disclosure of numeric **wild HP**, Genetics, individualization or private producer evidence, nor removal of TASK-028's existing field masks. A redesign of bootstrap/current-state HP visibility is deferred until **after MVP, only if subsequently needed**; no additional product decision on this known inference blocks the MVP. Security review must still verify the stated disclosure boundary and the feed stays **disabled** until immutable owned-origin evidence, deterministic replay, operational bounds and the remaining Class-A/enablement gates pass. Masking an event's `hpChange` is not a claim of cross-snapshot non-inference.
- Inter-Battle cadence changes, manual item use, reward, capture, policy transitions, retreat and recovery are **not** CombatEvents. SPEC-015's current Team HP and command responses remain the separate authoritative surfaces for those facts.

## 3. Authenticated read

`GET /player/hunts/:huntId/presentation?limit=64`

Optional query: `cursor=<opaque>`. `limit` is a decimal integer in `1..128`, defaults to `64` on an initial read, and caps the **total CombatPresentationEvent** entries in one response, counting `BattleStarted`. An issued cursor binds its effective `limit`; cursor reads that omit `limit` retain the bound value, while a different supplied value fails `400 invalid_request`. The encoded cursor is restricted to canonical unpadded **base64url** ASCII and at most **4096 bytes** before any parsing, cryptography or database access. Decoded fields have fixed lengths/depth; unknown signing-key identifiers fail closed, and signature verification is bounded and timing-safe. Oversized, malformed, tampered or otherwise invalid tokens fail with a generic `400 invalid_request` only after normal authentication/security checks, without revealing Hunt ownership. One response has at most four Battle envelopes and 256 KiB of UTF-8 JSON, including envelope metadata. An individual bootstrap and its first event must fit; if not, the source is unavailable rather than truncated into an invalid envelope. An individual continuation event that cannot fit even alone within the response ceiling likewise returns `410 presentation_unavailable`; the producer must reject oversize projected events before advertising a complete prefix. A response never splits an individual projected event. Unknown query fields, noncanonical Hunt UUIDs, malformed limits and malformed cursors are rejected.

The endpoint requires the ordinary authenticated ADR-006/SPEC-011 session, exact allowed credentialed Origin/CORS behavior and the Account-to-Player lookup from SPEC-015 `4.1`. The path `huntId` is only an opaque selector; the server applies Player ownership to every Hunt/checkpoint/evidence lookup. A foreign Hunt and an absent Hunt yield the same `404 not_found`. `GET` uses `touchActivity=false`, needs no CSRF or `Idempotency-Key` mutation header, and must not freeze a cutoff, advance a Hunt, trigger captures, process pending heals, grant rewards or modify command replay state. Send `Cache-Control: private, no-store` and do not expose the payload through shared caches.

The default read begins at the earliest available Battle bootstrap within the retained Hunt presentation window. A caller-provided cursor resumes a particular authorized stream position; the client cannot request arbitrary source events or issue a public owned-HP/bootstrap snapshot.

```json
        "sourceCombatEventSchemaVersion": "opaque pinned source version",
        "battleId": "opaque",
        "initialSides": [],
        "initialParticipants": [],
        "events": []
      }
    }
  ],
  "hasMore": true,
  "nextCursor": "opaque",
  "resumeCursor": null

```

The empty arrays above indicate only the JSON **shape**, not an admissible real bootstrap. Each actual `presentation` value MUST satisfy TASK-028's full exported runtime contract and contain its actual authoritative events; a bootstrap MUST contain at least its `BattleStarted` origin. Although TASK-028's pure continuation projector accepts an empty source slice, this HTTP feed MUST NOT publish a continuation with `events=[]`. Every nonempty `battles[]` entry advances at least one actual CombatEvent from that Battle, and elements are ordered by the accepted source timeline. `committedLogicalTimeMs` is a **decimal-integer string** within the existing safe logical-time domain, reflecting the committed presentation snapshot, not elapsed wall-clock time. A read must never extrapolate beyond committed checkpoint/effect boundaries. `snapshotId` is an opaque presentation-versioned commitment to an immutable committed prefix; it conveys no RNG, stats or internal DB identifiers.

`hasMore=true` requires a non-null `nextCursor`, `resumeCursor=null`, at least one nonempty Battle envelope and a **strictly advanced** logical stream position compared with its input cursor (or the origin sentinel for a first-page read). `hasMore=false` requires `nextCursor=null` and may return a non-null `resumeCursor` for an active Hunt, permitting polling for later committed events. A terminal and completely exhausted stream returns both cursors null. An empty `battles` page is permitted only when polling at the current committed end or when no Battle has yet been published; its stream watermark and cursors must still be coherent. Before any Battle, a cursor uses a distinct authenticated, version-bound **before-first-Battle sentinel** instead of a guessed ordinal/ID/sequence; on successful resume it advances from that sentinel to the actual first bootstrap only when it is committed. No fabricated bootstrap, empty continuation, duplicate event or unchanged nextCursor position is allowed to fill or extend a page.

## 4. Stable pagination and cursor semantics

Each first-page request freezes an immutable **published prefix** and `snapshotId` covering the exact presentation/source schema versions, Hunt identity, highest committed source event, committed logical-time metadata and terminal flag at read time. Repeated requests with the same cursor while its source is retained return identical stream positions, metadata and event bytes. Concurrent mutation can append new history but cannot rewrite a frozen published prefix. A successful empty polling response and a terminalization commit may advance snapshot metadata even when no new CombatEvent exists; `snapshotId` must therefore bind a publication generation, not only the last event identity.

`nextCursor` and `resumeCursor` are opaque, tamper-evident, server-authored capabilities. Their internal binding includes: Player identity, Hunt identity, concrete presentation schema version, pinned `gameDataVersion`/`rulesVersion`/`combatEventSchemaVersion`, immutable projection generation, published-prefix event index and digest, frozen logical-time/terminal metadata, stream position `(encounterOrdinal, encounterId, battleId, lastSequence, lastCombatTimeMs)`, expiry and cursor purpose. The projection generation must be separate from the mutable checkpoint OCC row version. A versioned authenticated cursor signature includes a key identifier; retired signing keys remain verifiable for at least the issued cursor's remaining 15-minute lifetime, subject to source retention, and are never sent to the client. No client-derived identity or time may substitute for this binding. Identity mismatch, wrong Hunt, unsupported version, expiration, source retention loss or invalid signature fails closed. A cursor from one Player is never valid for another, including under unchanged session/cookie shape.

A `nextCursor` continues **only** the frozen snapshot; it may not include an event committed after that snapshot. A `resumeCursor` requests a new stable committed prefix from the previously consumed end; promotion to the new watermark is permitted only when the full previously published prefix is provably byte-identical and all original pins remain retained. Otherwise the endpoint returns `409 snapshot_changed` and the client must begin a new bootstrap session, replacing its display state. A successful resume never republishes the already acknowledged `(Battle,sequence)` identity and emits the next Battle's bootstrap when appropriate.

All pages recheck ownership and retention. The server can return a smaller page to respect response-byte and envelope ceilings but may not skip events to satisfy them. Cursor lifetime is **15 minutes** after issue as the accepted v1 public limit; expiration is deterministic and never extends authoritative source retention. Pagination is read-only and independent of the idempotency keys for Hunt mutations.

## 5. Historical retention and unavailability

The accepted v1 presentation window covers active Hunts and terminal Hunts for **30 days from the first committed terminalization transaction's recorded database wall-clock time**, contingent on retained immutable input, event/presentation evidence, origin-owned-Shiny values, source versions and all other replay dependencies. The presentation store freezes its own `presentationTerminalRecordedAt` from `statement_timestamp()` at the terminalization statement, atomically with the terminal row; a rolled-back transaction leaves no retention anchor, and a replay cannot shift it. The timestamp is a transactionally committed **record of terminalization**, not PostgreSQL's separately observable exact commit timestamp. Do **not** use `solo_hunts.terminal_at` as the public retention anchor: that existing value is the checkpoint's logical-time wall-clock anchor and can predate a delayed bounded-`202` terminalization commit. It continues to govern SPEC-015 recovery without semantic change. The 30-day public window does not shorten SPEC-002/ADR-004 retention of historical authoritative game data/rules/event codecs needed elsewhere or the independent public-command replay/tombstone windows. Expiration of the public presentation window returns `410 presentation_expired` after the Hunt's ownership is established; foreign/absent resources still return `404`.

Retention is **not a license to reconstruct absent history**. Existing Hunts for which the exact initial owned-Shiny fact, roster, HP carry, event sequence or version cannot be proved return `410 presentation_unavailable`, even inside the window. A migration may add historical projection records only from independently verifiable immutable source evidence, with equality and provenance tests. Current Collection Pokémon data, latest catalog versions, current checkpoint HP, hard-coded `shiny=false` and visual heuristics are insufficient. No destructive rewrite of existing Hunt snapshots or schema-version reinterpretation is allowed.

The accepted v1 source is an **immutable, indexed, transactionally published projection ledger**, not a GET-time replay of the growing Hunt prefix. A server-private Battle-origin sidecar is separate from TASK-028 privacy-filtered public event bytes and battle headers. Both are linked to the same committed Hunt prefix; GET reads only indexed public projection rows and their retained version metadata. Addition of frozen owned-Shiny to new Hunt input authority and checkpoints requires a **versioned, additive** input/checkpoint schema (plus a compatible forward-only database migration). Old V1/V2 checkpoint readers preserve their exact original semantics. Never silently add `shiny` to an existing strict checkpoint schema or backfill from mutable Collection state. The additive schema and retention **strategy** are accepted at Class A; the numerical producer/read workload and retained-storage budgets are measured, reviewed and locked during the separate implementation/enablement gate before public reads become available.

## 6. Origin-evidence prerequisite

Before any producer publishes a Battle bootstrap, it must prove all of:

1. **Original Team identity:** start-transaction-frozen ordered Pokémon-instance roster, their exact form-aware species and levels, owned Shiny for every member, and original active versus reserve membership for that Battle. Owned Shiny must be frozen independently of the reusable Team or later Collection mutations.
2. **Exact battle-origin HP:** original owned `currentHp`/`maxHp` per combatant and original side membership from deterministic inter-Battle carry and already-applied explicit heals. The origin is the **pre-reaction `BattleInitInput`** (including cadence-bound carried `startingHp`, derived maximum HP, side roster and `initialActiveCombatantIds`), or an equivalent core-authored immutable sidecar captured before any `battleStart` effects. `initializeBattle` emits `BattleStarted` at sequence 1 and then resolves `battleStart` Ability reactions before returning its `state`, so the returned `battle.combatants` may already differ at time zero. Bootstrap must represent the pre-reaction values and include the ordered time-zero reaction events; reading post-reaction state as the origin would double-apply or conceal those effects. Last-committed `GET /player/hunts/state` Team HP is also not necessarily battle-origin HP.
3. **Wild individual:** the exact Encounter individualization snapshot/commitment and original visible `shiny`, never a fresh species roll or newly derived wild individual.
4. **Event provenance:** original Battle ID, original `BattleStarted`, source event schema, ordered normalized stimuli, RNG origin/continuation, initial policy/cadence, exact pinned rules and content, and any intervention needed to reproduce the same sequences, event payloads, KO/replacement and terminal result.
5. **Immutability:** the evidence survives direct versus segmented advancement, checkpoint/claim retries, process restart, overlapping command completion and terminalization without reroll or reinterpretation.

Current implementation evidence is **not yet a publication proof**. The Solo Hunt runtime retains completed-Encounter normalized stimuli/provenance, current Battle state/stimuli, deterministic origins and recorded inter-Battle healing; its replay validation re-executes history to compare terminal/current state. That helper does not currently yield a durable public event feed or preserve a ready-to-publish pre-reaction initial snapshot. `SoloHuntTeamMemberSnapshot` and the current frozen input/checkpoint schema omit owned Shiny even though the locked original Collection Team read exposes it. The frozen runtime-input envelope is currently v1 and Solo Hunt checkpoint is v2; the source must introduce a new **additive version** of each and keep old decoders unchanged rather than extending their strict allowed fields. Existing HTTP `GET /player/hunts/state` exposes latest committed Team HP and current visible Encounter, not TASK-028 bootstrap or any public combat cursor. These gaps must be corrected and separately tested before the endpoint is enabled.

### 6.1 Proposed additive origin versions

- New Starts use `hunt-runtime-inputs-v2` with required boolean `team[].shiny` frozen from the locked original Collection Team read, and `pokenexus.solo-hunt-checkpoint.v3` with the matching pinned Team identity. Their decoders validate the exact new nested shape, source-version binding and equality with the checkpoint schema-version column. Start, advancement, claim/prelude, explicit healing and pending-capture cleanup select a writer by the **persisted Hunt input/checkpoint version**, never by a guessed field or latest code default. Existing v1 input and v1/v2 checkpoint decoder/write paths keep their canonical semantics, including previously accepted legacy v1-to-v2 healing/provenance handling; none is silently promoted to v3.
- The deterministic core captures a validated private Battle-origin sidecar **after cadence-carried HP has been applied but before `battleStart` reactions**, preserving ordered sides/active memberships, original combatant IDs and instance bindings, exact owned HP/maxHP, pinned owned and wild Shiny, stable Encounter/Battle identities and source-version pins. The public bootstrap is projected from this sidecar plus the authentic `BattleStarted` and ordered time-zero events. A post-reaction `BattleState`, latest Team HP or mutable Collection read cannot substitute. The original initialized event array and all subsequently emitted event arrays must be available to the writer; replay equality must compare the full source CombatEvent sequence, not only end state and stimuli.
- An older Hunt without independently provable original owned Shiny and pre-reaction origin remains playable under its existing rules but returns `410 presentation_unavailable`. No automatic backfill or public-feed participation is inferred from a successful legacy checkpoint decode.

## 7. Bounded work and transactional publication

An authenticated GET must have a fixed server-side work ceiling covering source reads, validation, pagination and serialization, independent of Hunt duration and whether the requested page is early or late in an extensive history. The **writer** also needs a fixed per-invocation ceiling for raw projected-event count/bytes, private provenance generation, database rows/bytes and transaction duration. A single SPEC-015 checkpoint/claim `202` segment is not by itself an event-count bound: one Battle can produce many CombatEvents before the Encounter or cutoff boundary. The new backend must introduce deterministic, restart-safe **mid-Battle checkpoint yield points between complete core stimuli** when that projection budget would be exceeded; a single stimulus and the atomic time-zero Start event group cannot be partially applied or truncated. Every yielded state/event prefix is committed together under the same Hunt-command correlation and returned as bounded `202` continuation when work remains. If one indivisible source event/group cannot be projected within the accepted hard budget, gameplay remains governed by SPEC-015 and the Hunt's presentation source fails closed with a durable unavailability reason; it must neither silently omit events nor turn an otherwise admissible Hunt into a permanently unstartable one. The precise finite writer CPU/row/byte ceilings and failure/recovery tests require independent benchmark acceptance before implementing the bounded writer and enabling the endpoint. Public response limits in `3` are immediate hard ceilings. **The concrete producer and reader CPU/time/input-byte, storage-growth and retained-history limits must be benchmarked and accepted as part of the implementation enablement gate** against the longest supported production Hunt, rather than inferred from a small fixture. The selected indexed-read strategy deliberately excludes GET-time checkpoint decode, historical Battle replay and prefix scan.

The accepted indexed producer/reader contract is:

1. **Identity, private origin and index:** a Player/Hunt-scoped stream header pins input/checkpoint, presentation, game-data/rules and source CombatEvent versions; one immutable public Battle-origin header has `UNIQUE(huntId, encounterOrdinal)` and unique `(huntId, encounterId)`/`(huntId, battleId)` bindings to the same `(encounterOrdinal, encounterId, battleId)`, with full identity/byte equality on duplicate replay. One immutable projected event row has `UNIQUE(huntId, battleId, sequence)` **and `UNIQUE(huntId, eventIndex)`**, indexed seek on `(huntId, eventIndex)`, a monotonically increasing gap-free per-Hunt `eventIndex`, canonical public bytes, byte length and a rolling **public-byte-only** prefix commitment stored at each index. A separate immutable, server-private origin/source commitment binds the canonical **unprojected** CombatEvent bytes, private pre-reaction roster/HP sidecar, producer continuation context and exact source-version pins. This commitment is never emitted as a public snapshot ID, cursor field or response metadata. Retry/replay checks compare both private-source commitments and the entire existing public event identity/bytes: two different masked source events can project to identical public bytes, but must never pass source equality. A mismatch fails closed instead of rewriting history. The append watermark advances only across a validated contiguous prefix. Command `Idempotency-Key` deduplication does not replace event uniqueness.
2. **Projection, commit and visibility:** the source writer invokes the existing TASK-028 bootstrap/continuation projectors using authentic raw CombatEvents and a private, validated origin/continuation context, then stores the immutable **already privacy-projected public** Battle headers and event bytes together with the private source commitment. The GET may group and validate these stored public fields into TASK-028 envelopes, but must not replay raw CombatEvents, reinterpret a later checkpoint, or call a source projector against projected event bytes. The first Battle origin and **every wrapped CombatEvent** in `createSoloHuntRuntime().events` (including `BattleStarted`, any time-zero Ability reactions and any time-zero `BattleEnded`) publish in the same successful Start transaction as its v3 checkpoint/input authority; non-combat Solo Hunt simulation events are never projected into this feed. Later projected events and Battle origins publish only in the transaction whose checkpoint OCC write succeeds; discarded/stale attempts have no visible rows. A budget-induced safe mid-Battle yield is another checkpoint/OCC and projected-prefix transaction under that same command, never a projection-only write or invented Battle boundary. An indivisible oversize projection records an immutable source-unavailable disposition in the same authoritative transaction and exposes no synthetic, truncated or apparently complete transcript. Battle N may publish its committed ending before Encounter N effects finish. Battle N+1's bootstrap remains blocked until Encounter N has a committed reward/capture disposition and *all* due heals at that boundary have been applied or recorded not-applied. A boundary marked `committed` with scheduled heals still pending is insufficient. The writer rechecks this ordering under the existing Player/Hunt lock, including zero-gap transitions and crash/retry. No projection-only transaction may make an event visible before its source checkpoint/effect transaction.
3. **Terminal and failure:** completion of a terminal Hunt is a committed `solo_hunts` row fact, potentially separate from `SoloHuntRuntimeState.status`. The terminal writer verifies the entire preceding event prefix and **atomically seals** the terminal presentation flag, final generation/watermark and `presentationTerminalRecordedAt` **in the very same transaction** that first sets the authoritative `solo_hunts` terminal row. A replay cannot move the terminal anchor, and a committed terminal Hunt is never exposed as a still-active presentation snapshot. If the prefix cannot be proven, the transaction instead records the durable `presentation_unavailable` disposition alongside the valid terminal gameplay effect; it must not commit an unsealed terminal row with an apparently complete public prefix or require an unbounded later repair. Terminal exhaustion returns null cursors only for the atomically sealed terminal snapshot. A **recoverable transaction or publisher failure** rolls back the attempted checkpoint and projection writes together, retaining the prior committed event prefix for a bounded same-key retry; a temporarily incomplete projection remains behind its last committed watermark (`503` when the incomplete state cannot safely be served). A **deterministic, irrecoverable presentation-only failure** (including a single indivisible oversized event group, missing immutable presentation origin or source/projection collision) records a durable, idempotent Hunt-level `presentation_unavailable` disposition **atomically with the legitimate gameplay checkpoint, Start or terminal consequence**; the Hunt may continue under SPEC-015 without synthetic/truncated events, and all subsequent first-page, existing `nextCursor` and `resumeCursor` reads fail `410 presentation_unavailable` after confirming ownership. No previous partial prefix may be presented as a complete history and no unavailable marker may be silently cleared or rebuilt from mutable state. An authority failure that also prevents an otherwise valid gameplay transition follows the existing SPEC-015 rollback/error rules rather than fabricating gameplay progress. The independent audit must prove correct distinction between recoverable and permanent failures, race-safe transaction ordering and restart/retry identity.
4. **Bounded read:** one consistent database snapshot (`REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY` or a genuinely single-statement equivalent; the existing generic `withTransaction` defaults to `READ COMMITTED`) fetches the frozen stream header, at most the requested `limit` plus one event lookahead by indexed seek, and at most four Battle headers plus one lookahead. It groups actual projected bytes into TASK-028 bootstrap/continuation envelopes without scanning earlier Events, caps the full serialized response at 256 KiB and never skips an event or emits an empty continuation. A 15-minute signed cursor freezes its publication generation, index, **public-only** digest, terminal metadata and source pins. Repeated `nextCursor` pages retain the same snapshot; `resumeCursor` promotes only after the old prefix digest/versions verify unchanged. The initial no-events cursor is an authenticated before-first-Battle sentinel. Late-page work remains bounded by the indexed read, but operational latency/input-byte ceilings and cursor signing/key-rotation tests still require measured conformance evidence.

A budget exhaustion may return `503 presentation_temporarily_unavailable` and is never a partial event page marked complete. If a source version/individualization key is irrecoverable, return `410 presentation_unavailable` rather than fall back to latest. The presentation read itself never reconciles Encounter effects or heals.

## 8. Error and privacy contract

All non-200 responses use SPEC-015's JSON error envelope; do not serialize SQL, checkpoint bytes, replay origins, RNG, token contents or security internals. Permitted public distinctions are:

| HTTP | Code | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 400 | `invalid_request` | Bad canonical Hunt selector, unsupported query field, malformed cursor syntax or invalid `limit`. |
| 401 | `unauthorized` | Missing/expired session under existing authentication behavior. |
| 403 | `forbidden` | Existing Origin/security policy rejects the request; never use this to disclose Hunt ownership. |
| 404 | `not_found` | Hunt missing or not owned; intentionally indistinguishable. |
| 409 | `snapshot_changed` | Previously frozen prefix cannot safely be resumed after intervening history/version change. |
| 410 | `cursor_expired` | Validly authenticated cursor exceeded its public TTL. |
| 410 | `presentation_expired` | Owned Hunt is outside its public retention window. |
| 410 | `presentation_unavailable` | Immutable origin or a retained version required to prove that owned Hunt's transcript is missing. |
| 503 | `authority_unavailable` | A required temporary retained authority/codec is unavailable. |
| 503 | `presentation_temporarily_unavailable` | Bounded serving budget or transient evidence publication is incomplete. |

Invalid signatures, foreign cursor identities and opaque-cursor mismatches must not expose existence of any other Player's Hunt or retained history. Consumer code validates every runtime envelope and fails closed on a schema, Battle identity, sequence, timestamp or ownership mismatch. Renderers never manufacture source events or disclose hidden values; Card live announcements deduplicate by `(huntId, battleId, sequence)` and announce only newly committed events, not bootstrapped history replay.

## 9. Independent conformance and enablement matrix

An independent QA/security/persistence review must establish, using real engine-produced source evidence rather than renderer fixtures:

- **Origin parity:** for each Battle, the exact Team roster, active/reserve set, owned Shiny, **pre-reaction original owned HP** and wild Shiny reproduce under source replay; compare first Battle, a later Battle after carried damage, multiple explicit heals, inter-Battle cadence KO, same-Battle forced replacement and zero-gap transitions. Include a `battleStart` Ability that modifies owned HP at time zero and assert its pre-reaction bootstrap HP plus the exact subsequent reaction events, without deriving origin HP from `initializeBattle(...).state`.
- **Event parity:** exact `BattleStarted` once per Battle, exhaustive TASK-028 event projection, contiguous sequence/monotonic battle time, order across equal timestamps, no wild numeric leakage, and byte-equal **canonical bootstrap plus concatenated event sequence** for one uninterrupted advancement versus many checkpoint/claim segments, idempotent retries and hard restarts. Envelope page grouping may differ while the canonical participant origins and event sequence remain identical.
- **Commit visibility:** paused/incomplete reward/capture boundary, waiting heal, zero-gap next-Battle admission, concurrent checkpoint/claim/retreat and racing GET pagination do not publish uncommitted or reordered events; a stable cursor neither duplicates nor skips events.
- **Historical variants:** retained old rules/event-schema releases, current and terminal Hunts, retreat/no-living/opponent-victory/draw Battle evidence, source retention expiry, missing owned-Shiny pin, provenance corruption, failed historic backfill and unavailable individualization authority all have explicit fail-closed results.
- **Auth/privacy:** self-scoped session/Origin/CORS behavior, `touchActivity=false`, malformed/foreign/replayed/expired/tampered cursor handling, cookie/session rotations, cross-Player selector indistinguishability, no wild HP/Genetics/RNG/checkpoint leakage, and no GET gameplay mutation. Test **both** consecutive exact owned-HP Battle bootstraps and SPEC-015 committed owned-HP state reads against the timeline: zero-gap one-hit difference (100→87), multiple consecutive hits, item healing, multi-page reads and fixed-cutoff/segmented advancement.
