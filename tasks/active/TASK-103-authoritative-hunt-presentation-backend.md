## In scope

1. Preserve strict historical decoders/writes and already-produced TASK-103 evidence. Historical v1 behavior remains replayable under its pinned authority; missing immutable old evidence continues to fail closed rather than being fabricated.
2. For forward Hunts, capture a game-core-private authoritative Battle origin after cadence-carried state materialization and before `battleStart` reactions, using the accepted persisted starting/current HP authority from SPEC-021 rather than reinitializing owned Pokémon to max HP. 3. Make complete original source CombatEvent streams and replay comparison available at accepted safe boundaries. Deterministic bounded continuation may yield only between complete stimuli and must preserve the accepted automatic Potion/Revive/cadence ordering supplied by the upstream authoritative Hunt implementation.
4. Add only forward-compatible private origin/source commitments and a separate append-only, indexed **public privacy-projected** Battle-header/event ledger. Require immutable event `(huntId,battleId,sequence)` and `(huntId,eventIndex)` uniqueness, unique Battle ordinal/encounter/Battle mappings, contiguous public watermark, public-only prefix digest, independently checked private raw-source equality and canonical byte-idempotent retry. Existing TASK-028 projectors execute **on authentic raw events at the writer**, not on already-projected GET values.
5. Commit each Battle origin, event slice and prefix watermark in the same authoritative transaction as the corresponding accepted Hunt authority update; discarded attempts yield no public rows. Normal winning Encounters still settle their due capture/reward/automation/cadence work before Battle N+1. The Human-approved D-F16 B path is different: `BattleEnded(draw)` alone is insufficient, and Battle N+1 may publish only after atomic committed `resolved_non_win` + durable `PostBattleReviveApplied` + consumed PendingEncounterSelection provenance + all due automation/cadence settlement, with **no fabricated capture/reward row required**.
6. Recoverable writer/SQL failures roll back source+presentation together; deterministically irrecoverable presentation-only failures atomically record a permanent unavailable marker with legitimate gameplay progress. All first-page/next/resume reads of an owned permanently unavailable stream return `410 presentation_unavailable`. No partial feed may masquerade as complete history or silently resume after an unprovable prefix.
7. Implement authenticated self-scoped read-only GET (`touchActivity=false`), exact session/Origin/CORS behavior, `404` foreign/absent indistinguishability, no gameplay side effects, privacy-safe fields and `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. Read a single consistent DB snapshot using `REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY` or a genuinely single-statement equivalent; use bounded indexed seek rather than replaying Hunt history.
8. Implement immutable 15-minute signed cursors, bounded canonical unpadded base64url ingress `<=4096` bytes, key ID/rotation, Player/Hunt/version/public-prefix bindings, empty-before-first sentinel, stable nextCursor and safe resumeCursor promotion. Enforce at most 128 events, four Battle envelopes, 256 KiB serialized JSON, one event/header lookahead and fail-closed oversized public projection; keep exact source and public version pins.
9. Preserve all documented privacy limits: no numeric wild HP, Genetics, RNG, source origin or private commitment in public responses. The Human Owner accepted inference of **owned Pokémon** damage across exact owned-HP snapshots for MVP only; no additional wild-vitality/Genetics disclosure is authorized.
10. Keep public Battle activity and Hunt activity distinct. Historical `pokenexus.combat-presentation.v1` bytes are immutable. Forward in-Battle `CombatantRevived` plus deterministic `EffectRemoved`/`StatStageChanged` cleanup uses additive `pokenexus.combat-presentation.v2`. Post-Battle Revive, Inventory consumption, capture/reward disposition and compact resolved-Encounter facts are versioned Hunt activity and never append to an ended Battle transcript.
11. Preserve the exact immutable replay continuity accepted by SPEC-020 for post-Battle Revive, including `pendingSelectionIdentity`, consumed PendingEncounterSelection snapshot/commitment, before/after selection RNG provenance and pinned content/rules/individualization authority. Retry/reconnect/offline reads must reuse persisted provenance rather than reconstructing from mutable content.
