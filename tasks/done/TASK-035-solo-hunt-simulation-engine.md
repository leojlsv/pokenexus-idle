## Objective

Implement the deterministic, event-driven Solo Hunt simulation/orchestration core defined by APPROVED
SPEC-013. The implementation must compose the existing universal Combat Engine and cadence evaluator
rather than create parallel combat math, and it must produce deterministic Hunt/Encounter state and
immutable source evidence suitable for downstream reward/capture/checkpoint/persistence tasks.

The first production consumer is the exact TASK-091/095 production combat release paired with
`game-data-core-kanto-johto-v3`; retained historical contexts remain explicit and fail closed rather
than falling back to latest/current authority.

## Context / inherited authority

- ADR-002: Solo Hunt is elapsed/event driven; there is no persistent realtime server tick.
- ADR-004 / SPEC-003 / TASK-009: one universal deterministic Combat Engine owns damage, healing,
  effect scheduling/ticks, KO, initiative, Battle outcome and forced-replacement legality.
- SPEC-013: one Hunt is one cadence-continuity scope; HP, actor GCD, per-Move readiness, ordered Move
  cursor and cadence-scoped effects can cross Battle boundaries, while Battle-local state resets.
- SPEC-013: in-Battle `replacementPending` is resolved at zero combat time by selecting the first
  living eligible pinned Team reserve in Team order.
- SPEC-013: inter-Battle cadence effects can KO a Pokémon; the next Battle uses the first living
  eligible pinned Team member, or the Hunt auto-terminalizes at the exact no-living boundary.
- SPEC-013: only player-side sole victory successfully completes an Encounter; opposing-side victory
  or draw grants no encounter-completion reward/capture evidence and leaves the pending encounter
  selection unconsumed.
- TASK-034: immutable v3 Zone/Hunt/Encounter content is canonical; Verdant Edge currently supplies the
  first production sample and the configured Hunt recovery input.
- TASK-091/095: production Move/Ability support and exact v3 combat-rule binding are canonical; no
  unsupported Move/Ability semantics may be invented by the Hunt layer.

## Scope

### 1. Deterministic Hunt runtime model

- define pure game-core runtime types for one pinned Solo Hunt cadence:
  - stable Hunt identity and pinned Zone/Hunt/content/context identities supplied by the caller;
  - ordered pinned player Team snapshot references required by orchestration;
  - current logical Hunt time;
  - encounter ordinal/current Encounter runtime;
  - deterministic encounter/policy RNG continuation that is explicitly separate from the Combat
    Engine `DeterministicState.rng`; Hunt selection/policy must never consume combat RNG;
  - cadence carry returned by the shared Combat Engine;
  - ordered Move-sequence cursor per participating cadence identity;
  - immutable completed-encounter/source evidence generated by simulation;
  - terminal status/reason and pending post-encounter lifecycle handoff markers required by SPEC-013.
- keep infrastructure timestamps, SQL/OCC/idempotency-key representation and durable codecs out of
  game-core; those remain TASK-037/038.
- initialize a genuinely fresh Hunt cadence exactly as SPEC-013 requires:
  - every pinned player Team member starts at full derived HP;
  - no prior-Hunt cadence effects or action locks survive;
  - actor GCD and every per-Move cooldown start ready;
  - every ordered Move-sequence cursor starts at slot 1;
  - no incomplete prior Battle runtime, Battle-local stat stage/effect state or opponent cadence state
    is imported into the new Hunt.

### 2. Deterministic encounter selection

- consume caller-supplied immutable/versioned Encounter definitions and weights from the pinned Hunt
  content context;
- select encounter definition and level deterministically from explicit RNG state;
- preserve stable ordinal/identity inputs supplied/derived by the simulation contract;
- support a caller-supplied unresolved `PendingEncounterSelection` exact-context token without
  resampling it;
- fail closed on invalid/empty weights, invalid level bands or context mismatch rather than infer
  replacement/latest content.
- consume a caller-supplied immutable opponent-template catalog keyed by exact
  `(EncounterDefinitionId, Level)` and resolved for every selectable Encounter Species/Level under the
  exact pinned data/rules context. Templates contain Battle-init-ready factual/resolved fields but no
  occurrence-local Battle/Combatant/cadence identity or mutable HP/readiness/effect/cursor state;
  TASK-035 derives those fresh per Encounter occurrence. This catalog lets one pure cutoff advancement
  cross multiple Encounters without a mutable callback or `game-core -> game-data` dependency. TASK-035
  must not synthesize wild/NPC Move loadouts by reusing owned-Pokémon bootstrap ordering because no
  accepted NPC-loadout authority currently exists.

### 3. Battle construction and shared-engine orchestration

- preflight the full caller-resolved opponent-template catalog before any advancement, rejecting
  missing/duplicate/extra `(EncounterDefinitionId, Level)` keys, Species/Level/context mismatches or
  Battle-init-invalid templates atomically;
- construct Battle inputs from the pinned player Team, exact selected template and exact resolved
  production combat context supplied by the caller;
- use `initializeBattle`, `resolveCombatStimulus`, `createCadenceCarry` and `advanceCadence`
  from TASK-009; do not duplicate Combat Engine rules;
- one player Pokémon is active; ordered living pinned reserves remain Battle members for forced
  replacement;
- generate versioned `useMove` ActionIntents via the accepted deterministic ordered Move-sequence
  policy outside the Combat Engine;
- select deterministic legal targets only from engine-visible Battle state and pinned policy rules;
- resolve mandatory zero-time forced replacement before any later time or Move stimulus.
- where Hunt policy needs to ask whether a candidate Move/target is currently executable, expose/reuse
  one narrow read-only game-core legality query shared with the Combat Engine; do not copy the
  engine's private legality/target/effect-binding rules into Hunt code.

### 4. Ordered Move-sequence policy

- maintain one deterministic cursor for each pinned cadence participant;
- inspect the configured ordered loadout cyclically from the cursor;
- choose the first executable/legal ready Move at the current engine boundary according to the
  accepted SPEC-003 policy; unsupported/unresolvable choices are skipped, never synthesized;
- preserve cursor continuity across Battle boundaries in the same Hunt;
- fresh Hunt initialization resets the cursor; cross-Hunt persistence is out of scope.

### 5. Time advancement and inter-Battle cadence

- advance logical Battle time only to deterministic engine boundaries needed to make the next policy
  action legal/ready; no realtime polling/tick loop;
- after successful player-side Encounter completion, materialize cadence carry, prune every
  non-continuing opponent HP/max-HP/readiness/action-lock/cursor entry and every opponent-targeted
  cadence effect, while retaining all pinned player Team participants (including HP=0) and any
  player-targeted cadence effect regardless of its defeated source;
- advance an explicit pinned non-negative `interBattleGapMs` through `advanceCadence`; current
  production policy uses `0` because no non-zero inter-Battle gap has been authored. This value is
  distinct from TASK-034 `recoveryDurationMs`, which is post-Hunt recovery/setup downtime;
- process cadence-effect KO/expiry through the shared evaluator before constructing the next Battle;
- auto-terminalize at the exact no-living logical boundary and never create a new Battle afterward.

### 6. Encounter outcome mapping and immutable evidence

- map terminal `BattleEnded` exactly per SPEC-013:
  - player-side sole victory => successful Encounter completion with `completionKind = defeat`;
  - opposing-side victory or draw => no successful completion and terminal Hunt no-living outcome;
- on successful completion emit deterministic immutable encounter evidence containing at least Hunt
  identity, encounter ordinal/identity, selected encounter definition/species/level, participant
  identities, completion kind and the pinned reward-content envelope/reference needed by TASK-036/037;
- consume/advance the pending selection only after successful player-side completion;
- expose the post-Encounter capture-decision eligibility handoff without implementing capture
  probability, Ball debit or captured-Pokémon grant.

### 7. Deterministic stopping boundaries needed by downstream tasks

- provide a pure advancement API that can stop at an explicit caller-supplied logical cutoff;
- never generate a new automatic Move after the exact cutoff boundary;
- settle same-time mandatory replacement/lifecycle work required by SPEC-003 at the cutoff;
- do not implement command correlation/OCC persistence, but make the deterministic boundary replayable
  for TASK-037/038.

  orchestrate shared Combat Engine primitives only.

  dependent on infrastructure scheduling.

- A cutoff bug could grant an extra automatic action after a stop/checkpoint boundary; tests must cover
  exact equality boundaries and same-time replacement settlement.
- Encounter completion evidence must be immutable and exactly-once by deterministic identity, but this
  task does not claim durable exactly-once persistence authority.
- No irreversible operation is required. Commit/push/merge remain separately Human-gated.
